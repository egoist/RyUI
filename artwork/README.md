# Artwork

Icons and logos for Asuka, as SVG. Every piece of text is outlined, so no
font needs to be installed to show them.

- `icons/`: app icons, 1024×1024, clipped to a continuous-corner square.
  Some are built on the Λ of the ΛSUKΛ wordmark, `mei` and `warning` on
  明, the first character of 明日香, and the rest on scenes and objects
  (`pole`, `core`, `umbilical`, `sync`, `field`, `lance`).
- `logos/`: lockups of the wordmark, most in a dark and a light version.

Colors: red `#e3212b`, ink `#0e0d10`, paper `#f4efe5`, orange `#ff7a1a`.

The geometric ΛSUKΛ letters and the marks are drawn by hand. Other text is
outlined from typefaces under the SIL Open Font License:
[Shippori Mincho B1](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Shippori+Mincho+B1)
(the mincho Λ, a V turned over, and 明日香),
[Playfair Display](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Playfair+Display) (the
motto) and [Share Tech Mono](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Share+Tech+Mono)
(the seal's rim and the readouts).
